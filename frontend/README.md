# Silver Alpha // User Navigation Guide

Welcome to the **Silver Alpha High-Fidelity Asset & Surveillance Repository**. This guide provides instructions on how to navigate the cinematic standard of digital currency intelligence.

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: The Landing Deck
The entry point features the "Silver Alpha" high-fidelity asset.

*   **Inertial Navigation**: Use your **Mouse Scroll** or **Trackpad** to navigate.
    *   Scrolling down rotates the coin, revealing its machined detail and deep metallic reflections.
    *   The coin's movement is physics-based; faster scrolls result in more aggressive rotations.
*   **The Vault Entry**: Once you reach the bottom of the landing deck, click the **[ENTER REPOSITORY]** button to access the surveillance network.

---

## 🌍 Phase 2: Silver Surveillance Globe
The repository view provides a global overview of silver market intelligence.

*   **World Interaction**:
    *   **Click & Drag**: Rotate the Earth to view different geographical regions.
    *   **Scroll Zoom**: Zoom in to inspect terrestrial detail or zoom out for a tactical overview.
*   **Intelligence Nodes**: Pulsing **Neon/Red Pointers** indicate active market events.
    *   **Green Pointers**: Bullish market sentiment.
    *   **Red Pointers**: Bearish market sentiment.
    *   **Selection**: Click on any pointer to open the detailed **Analytical Intelligence Card**.

---

## 📊 Phase 3: Analytical Intelligence Card
Selecting a node focuses the system on a specific region and opens the analysis HUD.

*   **Dynamic Viewport**: Upon selection, the Earth will automatically shift to the left to provide a side-by-side view with the data card.
*   **Narrative Strength Curve**: 
    *   View the high-frequency jagged area graph representing the narrative power over time.
    *   Colors are sentiment-aware: **Green** for Bullish expansions, **Red** for Bearish contractions.
*   **Key Metrics**:
    *   **Trust Level**: Reliability rating of the incoming intelligence.
    *   **Action State**: Strategic directive (BUY / SELL / WATCH).
    *   **Indicators**: Visual progress bars representing Size, Velocity, Stability, and Source density.

---

## ⚓ Phase 4: System Reset
*   **Closing Analysis**: Click the **[×]** in the top-right of the Intelligence Card to return to the global rotation mode.
*   **Return to Deck**: Click the **[◄ RETURN TO DECK]** button in the bottom-left corner to exit the repository and return to the main Silver Alpha asset view.
